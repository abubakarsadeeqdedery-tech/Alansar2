About Our School

Welcome to AL'ANSAR ACADEMY DEDERI

Established in 2021, AL'ANSAR ACADEMY DEDERI is located in Dederi, Karaye Local Government, Kano State. We are committed to providing quality education that combines Islamic values with modern academic excellence.

With approximately 140 students across various classes from Pre-nursery to Basic 2, we focus on holistic development of every child under our care.
